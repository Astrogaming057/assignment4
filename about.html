I love dragons and dinos — favorite games and why

I'm drawn to immersive worlds where creatures feel real, where ecosystems and combat have weight. Games let me be both explorer and storyteller — whether I'm hunting, nesting, or just surviving with a pack.

  • The Isle — you'll find me on Evrima and Legacy. I love the tension and open-world survival; the dinosaur behavior and player-driven moments are unbeatable.
  • Day of Dragons — focused on dragon life, flight and the grind to mature into something lethal and beautiful.
  • Draconia — another dragon-centric world with an emphasis on growth and aerial combat.
  • Phasmophobia — cooperative horror with friends; great for tense streams and hilarious failures.
  • Beast of Bermuda — sandbox dinosaur-style gameplay and emergent player stories.
  • Path of Titans — deep dinos, customization, and slow-burn progression.
